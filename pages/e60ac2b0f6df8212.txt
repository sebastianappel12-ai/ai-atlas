VC funding news InfotechLead.com Zipline Raises $1.5 Billion at $21.5 Billion Valuation to Expand Autonomous Drone Delivery US autonomous drone delivery company Zipline disclosed $1.5 billion in financing on November 5, valuing the company at $21.5 billion post-money. Paradigm, WCM Investment Management and Layer Global led the investment, with participation from Uber, Tiger Global and J.P. Morgan. The valuation represents a substantial increase from the $7.6 billion valuation associated with its earlier $600 million financing. Founded in 2014 and led by CEO Keller Cliffton, Zipline operates autonomous delivery networks across four continents. The company transports medical supplies, consumer products and food. Funding will expand US manufacturing, recruitment and nationwide delivery infrastructure. Zipline is also developing its commercial delivery partnership with Uber. Humble Robotics Raises $51 Million to Develop Electric Autonomous Trucks for Regional Freight San Francisco-based autonomous vehicle startup Humble Robotics disclosed $51 million in financing on November 5, led by Obvious Ventures, bringing cumulative funding to $75 million. Founded by Eyal Cohen, previously associated with Apple, Uber and Waabi, Humble develops electric autonomous trucks without conventional driver cabins. Its Humble Hauler is designed to transport 40-foot shipping containers across regional freight routes generally shorter than 250 miles. The company plans initial customer pilots during early 2027, using conventional vehicles to collect operational data before introducing its purpose-built trucks. Commercial operations are targeted for the third quarter of 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. Funding will support vehicle engineering, testing and manufacturing development. Trajectory Raises $45 Million Series A at $300 Million Valuation for Enterprise AI Model Training Enterprise AI startup Trajectory announced a $45 million Series A on November 2, valuing the company at approximately $300 million. Sequoia Capital led the investment, with participation from Nvidia, Bessemer Venture Partners and Bain Capital Ventures. Founded by three Stanford University classmates with previous experience at Google DeepMind and Apple, Trajectory develops software that enables enterprises to improve AI models through feedback and automated training. The platform targets specialised applications in financial services, legal technology and other knowledge-intensive industries. Trajectory employs approximately 18 people. The investment will support product development, engineering recruitment and commercial expansion.
