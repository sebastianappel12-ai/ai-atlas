INDUSTRY GLOSSARY ELA: English Language Arts, a primary segment of the K-12 curriculum market. MPI: Management Performance Index, a data set used to track competitive market share in the higher education industry. Inclusive Access: A sales model where digital course materials are provided to students automatically, often through tuition or course fees. Evergreen: A digital delivery model that allows educational content and titles to be updated continuously rather than through traditional periodic editions. Agentic AI: Advanced artificial intelligence systems designed to autonomously interact with content and perform complex pedagogical tasks. Model Context Protocol (MCP): An emerging technology standard that allows AI models to securely interface with proprietary content and teaching workflows. RPO: Remaining Performance Obligation, representing the total value of contracted revenue that has not yet been recognized.
