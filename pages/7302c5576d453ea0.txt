AGCO Completes 150,000-Panel Solar Project in Spain Under 10-Year Power Agreement Agricultural equipment manufacturer AGCO announced completion of construction and commissioning at its Olmedo solar project in Valladolid, Spain, developed by renewable energy company BRUC. The installation comprises two solar projects, Los Silos and El Granero, covering approximately 300 hectares or 741 acres, with 150,000 solar panels. Under a 10-year virtual power purchase agreement signed in September 2025, AGCO expects to receive renewable electricity certificates corresponding to approximately 150 GWh of annual generation, equivalent to the electricity consumption of 40,000 European households. Electricity delivery to Spain’s grid is expected in the coming months, subject to regulatory approvals. The project supports AGCO’s target to reduce absolute Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas emissions by 55 percent by 2033 compared with 2022. HiTHIUM Unveils 6.9 MWh Battery Storage System with 8-Hour Duration and 96% Efficiency HiTHIUM showcased lithium-ion and sodium-ion energy storage systems covering 2–8 hours of discharge duration at RE+ 2026 in Las Vegas. Its ∞Power 6.9+ MWh system delivers eight-hour storage using 1,300+ Ah battery cells housed in a standard 20-foot container. The system offers a 25-year design life and at least 96 percent DC round-trip efficiency. Compared with a 5 MWh configuration, HiTHIUM claims 27 percent fewer containers and 68 percent lower peak auxiliary power consumption. Its ∞Power N4.0 MWh sodium-ion system uses 785 Ah cells, with up to 20,000 charge-discharge cycles and a 30-year design life. HiTHIUM also introduced a 1,022 kWh liquid-cooled battery cabinet and plans to complete a European pre-commissioning testing facility in Spain in 2027. Trina Storage Launches Hybrid Gas-Battery Power System Scalable from 5 MW to 1 GW for AI Data Centers Trina Storage unveiled its Elementa Hybrid Genset at RE+ 2026, combining gas-fired generation, battery energy storage and integrated power controls for AI data centers. The modular system supports installations ranging from 5 MW to 1 GW, using gas engines rated up to 2.5 MW and battery storage configurations based on 5 MW and 10 MW building blocks. Its architecture combines Elementa DC batteries, Electra AC systems and a hybrid plant controller. The battery system handles rapid AI workload fluctuations while gas generators provide sustained electricity. The solution supports four operating configurations: grid-connected backup, grid-constrained, bridge-to-grid and off-grid power. Two control architectures, Load Sensing and Double Conversion, are available. Trina Storage is continuing hardware-in-the-loop testing, grid-forming validation and certification activities.
