SUMMARY Diodes Incorporated (DIOD +13.17%) reported record financial results for the third quarter of 2026, driven by growth across automotive, industrial, and computing end markets. Management stated that sequential revenue growth was primarily supported by content gains in vehicles and momentum within the artificial intelligence ecosystem. The company reported that targeted capacity investments made over the previous year are now operational, which improved manufacturing facility loading and manufacturing efficiency. Management indicated that these operational gains, along with favorable product mix and pricing adjustments, contributed to gross margin expansion of 540 basis points year over year. Yang stated that artificial intelligence infrastructure complexity is expanding content opportunities in timing, connectivity, and storage, with AI ecosystem content now reaching $109 per unit. Yu noted that internal fabrication facilities in Scotland and South Portland are not yet fully loaded, providing potential for further utilization and margin gains as demand increases. Yang reported that Pericom product revenue has grown more than threefold since its acquisition, reaching a record level during the quarter. Yang stated that the acquisition of ElevATE Semiconductor allows the company to benefit from a growth cycle in the semiconductor automated test equipment market. Management stated that networking driven by artificial intelligence is creating new growth opportunities for 800-gigabit optical modules and the beginning of 1.6T ramps. Yang indicated that management intends to avoid repeating previous distribution challenges by monitoring channel inventory levels and point of sale trends closely. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY 1.6T: 1.6 terabits per second, a high-speed data transfer standard for next-generation networking equipment. ADAS: Advanced driver assistance systems are electronic technologies in vehicles that improve safety and assist the driver. ATE: Automated test equipment consists of computerized systems that perform tests on semiconductor devices to ensure functionality. NVMe: Non-volatile memory express is a communication protocol specifically designed for high-speed access to storage media. PCIe: Peripheral Component Interconnect Express is a high-speed interface standard for connecting hardware components like servers and storage. POS: Point of sale systems track when and where a customer makes a payment for a product or service. SiC: Silicon Carbide is a semiconductor material used in high-power and high-voltage applications such as electric vehicles and AI data centers.
