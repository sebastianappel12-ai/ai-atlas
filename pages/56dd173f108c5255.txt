INDUSTRY GLOSSARY 1.6T (1.6 Terabit): A high-speed optical transceiver capable of transmitting data at 1.6 trillion bits per second. 800G / 400G: Optical transceivers with data transmission speeds of 800 and 400 billion bits per second, respectively. CATV: Community Access Television, commonly referred to as cable television. CPO (Co-Packaged Optics): An advanced technology that integrates optical components and processors in a single package to improve efficiency. DSP (Digital Signal Processor): A specialized microprocessor used to process high-speed signals within optical modules. ELSFP (External Laser Small Form-factor Pluggable): A module format for external lasers used in co-packaged optics architectures. Hyperscale: Large-scale data centers typically operated by major technology firms like Amazon, Google, or Microsoft. Indium Phosphide: A semiconductor material used to manufacture high-performance lasers and photo detectors for optical fiber communications. LTA (Long-Term Agreement): A multiyear contractual commitment between a supplier and a customer regarding purchase volumes and pricing.
