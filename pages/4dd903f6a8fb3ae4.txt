INDUSTRY GLOSSARY GTV (Gross Transaction Value): The total dollar value of all real estate sales transactions closed by agents within a network. GCI (Gross Commission Income): The total commission generated from a real estate transaction before splits or expense deductions. Adjusted EBITDA: A non-GAAP measure of earnings that excludes interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, alongside specific merger or restructuring costs. Pro Forma: A financial calculation that presents results as if a merger or acquisition had been completed at the start of a comparison period. Home Platform: The proprietary cloud-based software suite that integrates CRM, marketing, and transaction management for Compass agents. AEO (Answer Engine Optimization): The process of optimizing website content to be accurately retrieved and surfaced by artificial intelligence assistants and AI-driven search tools. CSAT (Customer Satisfaction): A metric used to gauge how products or services provided by a company meet or surpass customer expectations.
