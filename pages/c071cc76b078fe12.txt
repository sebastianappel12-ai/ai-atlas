Here's My Nvidia Stock Prediction for 2027. Nvidia shareholders have been on a wild ride over the last couple of weeks. By Anthony Di Pizio – Nov 26, 2026 at 5:29AM EST +Fool.comonShare Summarize with AI Key Points Nvidia's longtime CEO, Jensen Huang, abruptly left the company on Nov. 12. Given Nvidia's importance to America's success in the global artificial intelligence (AI) race, President Trump intervened to have Huang reinstated on Monday. I think Huang's return sets the stage for significant upside in Nvidia stock in 2027. 10 stocks we like better than Nvidia › Nvidia (NVDA +0.80%) was founded in 1993 by a trio of engineers: Jensen Huang, Chris Malachowsky, and Curtis Priem. They shared a common goal of bringing 3D graphics to personal computers, paving the way for exciting new games and software applications. More than 30 years later, Nvidia's graphics processing units (GPUs) are not only the best in the world for gaming, but they have also been adapted for data centers, where they do most of the heavy lifting in artificial intelligence (AI) workloads. As the company's longtime CEO, Huang's vision has been instrumental to this shift, but he swiftly resigned on Nov. 12 after his marital infidelity came to light. Huang's shocking exit cast a cloud of uncertainty over Nvidia's future, and it contributed to a 48% decline in the company's stock. Nvidia's turmoil also posed a threat to America's leadership position in the global AI race, so President Donald Trump intervened, asking the board of directors to bring Huang back. The board announced his official reinstatement on Monday, much to the delight of investors. Nvidia stock is still down 31% from its peak (as of the market close on Nov. 23), but I'm predicting significant gains in 2027. Jensen Huang. Image source: Nvidia. Huang rejoins Nvidia at a critical moment In an interview with CNBC on Monday, President Trump said Huang is already "thinking three chips into the future," making him indispensable in America's mission to win the AI race. Nvidia recently started shipping its world-leading Vera Rubin systems, which include Rubin GPUs, Vera central processing units (CPUs), and a series of upgraded networking components. The Vera Rubin architecture can deliver up to 30 times more performance than Nvidia's previous Blackwell Ultra architecture, which could translate to a 97% reduction in inference token costs. Inference tokens are the text, images, or computer code generated by an AI model in response to a prompt, so in simple terms, Vera Rubin could dramatically lower the cost of running AI software. Lower costs have the potential to increase AI adoption while also making data center operators more profitable -- two things that could significantly increase demand for Nvidia's chips over time. Nvidia's interim CEO, Colette Kress, who is now expected to return to her role as chief financial officer, recently said Vera Rubin generated almost $20 billion in sales during Nvidia's fiscal 2027 third quarter (ended Oct. 25), making this the fastest product ramp-up in the company's history. But Huang has already laid out a product road map that includes the launch of Rubin Ultra in the second half of next year, followed by an entirely new architecture called Feynman in calendar 2028. In other words, President Trump wasn't kidding when he said Huang is thinking way ahead.
