SUMMARY Horizon Technology Finance Corporation (HRZN +0.00%) reported third quarter results where net investment income supported regular distributions despite a reduction in net asset value. Management reported an expansion of the origination pipeline and the fourth consecutive quarter of debt portfolio growth while addressing credit challenges in specific investments. The company executed share repurchases for more than 2 million shares, citing a difference between market valuation and its internal outlook. Strategic initiatives included the appointment of a Chief Growth and Strategy Officer and the utilization of co-investment capabilities with Monroe Capital. Chief Executive Officer Michael Balkin stated, "To support that effort, we are pleased to create the new role of Chief Growth and Strategy Officer at our adviser and to appoint Manuel Henriquez to this position." Paul Seitz noted that third quarter venture capital investments reached $98 billion, though he stated that the market is bifurcated with AI companies receiving the majority of capital. Management expects a lower level of loan prepayment activity in the fourth quarter of 2026 due to the current macroeconomic environment. The company amended two credit facilities during the quarter, extending legal final maturity dates to June 2034 and December 2035 respectively. The company's internal credit rating scale was realigned to a 1 to 5 scale to match the Monroe Capital platform, with 12% of the portfolio rated 4 or 5 at quarter end. Daniel Trolio reported that the company had no outstanding balance under its $150 million KeyBank credit facility as of Sept. 30, 2026. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY BDC: Business Development Company, a form of closed-end investment company that invests in small and mid-sized businesses. NAV: Net Asset Value, the total value of an entity's assets minus its total liabilities, often expressed on a per-share basis. NII: Net Investment Income, the income received from investment assets after deducting operating expenses. Backlog: The total amount of loans that have been committed, approved, or awarded but not yet funded. Onboarding Yield: The annualized yield on new debt investments added to the portfolio during a specific period. Spillover Income: Taxable income that a BDC has earned but not yet distributed to shareholders. Warrant: A derivative security that gives the holder the right to purchase the issuer's common stock at a specific price. PIK Interest: Payment-in-Kind interest, which is added to the principal balance of a loan rather than being paid in cash.
