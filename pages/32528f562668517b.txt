SUMMARY CION Investment Corporation (NYSE:CION) completed its balance sheet deleveraging strategy during the third quarter by reducing total principal debt to $897 million. Management reported the repayment of public Israeli bonds and the early termination of a senior secured credit facility, which brought the net debt-to-equity ratio to the low end of its targeted range. To support this deleveraging, the company limited investment activity to follow-on commitments and formed a new joint venture, Senior Loan Fund Partners, to which it sold $180 million in first-lien loans. Management stated that these actions, while impacting near-term earnings and resulting in a 17% reduction in the 2027 base distribution, were intended to strengthen the balance sheet for future share repurchases. Sale of Longview Power: Reisner noted the company expects significant gains upon the expected closing in the fourth quarter of 2026 or first quarter of 2027. Pearl standalone status: Bresner indicated that a signed nonbinding letter of intent for a business combination with K&N Growth Acquisition Corp. II aims to establish Pearl as a standalone public company. Shareholder return strategy: Reisner stated the company intends to prioritize the resumption of share repurchases using liquidity generated from the expected sale of Longview Power. Joint venture validation: Management noted that the sale of loans to the new joint venture at 99.8% of par served as a validator for the company's internal loan fair value marks. Portfolio risk profile: Bresner reported that over 99% of the company's PIK investments are held in first-lien assets, which management believes distinguishes the income from restructured debt. INDUSTRY GLOSSARY Business Development Company (BDC): A specialized investment vehicle designed to facilitate capital flow to small and medium-sized private companies. Senior Secured First-Lien Loan: Debt that is secured by a pledge of collateral and has the highest priority of repayment. Net Asset Value (NAV): The market value of a company's total assets minus its total liabilities. Net Investment Income (NII): The difference between a company's investment income and its operating expenses. Paid-In-Kind (PIK) Interest: A type of interest payment that is added to the principal balance of a loan rather than being paid in cash. Nonaccrual Status: A classification for a loan where interest is no longer being recognized because the borrower is significantly behind on payments. Secured Overnight Financing Rate (SOFR): A broad measure of the cost of borrowing cash overnight collateralized by Treasury securities. Amortized Cost: The amount at which an investment is acquired, adjusted for any discounts, premiums, or impairment.
