The Nasdaq plunged 5.52 per cent to close at 24,315.00, falling more than 10 per cent below the record level it had reached last month and entering correction territory. Investors turned cautious about technology companies after concerns emerged that Democratic gains in both chambers of Congress and key gubernatorial races could affect the expansion of data centres in the United States. On Wednesday, shares of major technology companies, including Amazon, Meta and Google, had rallied on expectations that new restrictions could reduce their spending on data centres and free up cash, potentially improving profitability. However, investors reversed those trades on Thursday amid concerns that the proposed restrictions could ultimately hurt the technology sector, CNN reported.
