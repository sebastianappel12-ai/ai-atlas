SUMMARY Savers Value Village, Inc. (NYSE:SVV) reported that financial results were supported by sustained comparable store growth in the United States and margin expansion in Canada. Management noted that the company is executing a technology-driven innovation strategy intended to improve labor efficiency and pricing accuracy across its retail footprint. The company stated that capital allocation remains focused on funding new store openings and debt reduction as the business approaches a net leverage target below 2.0x. While overall net sales increased, management indicated that year-over-year comparisons in the fourth quarter will be influenced by high performance benchmarks from the preceding year. Management announced the launch of Autotagger and image capture technology, with COO Tanious stating, "We are literally on the front end of rolling it this quarter" and targeting a full fleet rollout by early 2028. The company reported that the ThriftIQ pricing platform is now live in 108 stores, with Walsh noting, "ThriftIQ is now live in 108 stores" and has priced over 40 million items to date. Management indicated that ThriftIQ reduces the time required to train new store graders by approximately half, supporting faster profitability ramps for new locations. Walsh highlighted a shift in Canadian consumer trends, stating, "the lower household income cohort showed growth" during the quarter, which management described as a green shoot for the segment. The company noted that U.S. growth is being driven by a broad range of shoppers, with Walsh stating, "Younger and higher household income shoppers are still some of our highest-performing demographics." INDUSTRY GLOSSARY Adjusted EBITDA: A non-GAAP financial measure that excludes interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization, along with specific costs like debt extinguishment and stock-based compensation. Autotagger: A patent-pending industrial engineering solution designed to automate the tagging process for secondhand items to improve labor efficiency. Constant Currency: A financial calculation that translates current period results using prior period exchange rates to eliminate the impact of currency fluctuations. CPC: Centralized Processing Center; a facility used to process and price donated goods before they are sent to retail stores. GreenDrop: A service and location network used by the company to collect donations on behalf of its nonprofit partners. OSD: On-site Donations; items donated by consumers directly at a retail store location rather than through third-party partners. Sales Yield: A metric defined as retail sales generated per pound of merchandise processed on a currency-neutral and comparable store basis. ThriftIQ: The company's proprietary, AI-enabled pricing platform used to provide data-driven price recommendations for unique secondhand items.
