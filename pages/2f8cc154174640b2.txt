By Eric Volkman – Nov 25, 2026 at 6:27PM EST +Fool.comonShare Summarize with AI Key Points It also increased its revenue at a double-digit pace. Both key fundamentals beat analyst estimates. 10 stocks we like better than GDS Holdings › Tech stocks didn't do well in the last trading session before Thanksgiving. One exception was Chinese techie GDS Holdings (GDS -2.25%); its American Depositary Shares (ADSs) inched up marginally on a day when the S&P 500 index dipped by almost 0.3%. It managed to swim against the current due to its latest earnings release, which featured better-than-expected fundamentals. The shift is the thing GDS Holdings' third quarter saw the company reap 3.18 billion yuan ($477 million) in revenue, up 10% year over year. On the bottom line, the company flipped to a loss of 5.47 million yuan ($821 million) from the year-ago profit of 712.31 million yuan ($107 million) -- although the third-quarter 2025 figure was inflated by a one-time accounting gain of 1.37 billion yuan ($206 million). On a per-ADS basis, GDS' loss was 0.04 yuan ($0.01). Image source: Getty Images. Although no investor appreciates a tumble into the red, analysts tracking GDS were expecting far worse. Their estimate for net loss was 0.24 yuan ($0.04) per ADS. The company also beat the average pundit projection of 3.14 billion yuan ($471 million) for revenue. GDS is a company in transition, shifting from a cloud computing focus to a focus on operating artificial intelligence (AI) data centers. The company said new bookings and continued progress in getting its facilities onstream helped push revenue higher. Higher costs, primarily from data center build-outs, affected the bottom line.
