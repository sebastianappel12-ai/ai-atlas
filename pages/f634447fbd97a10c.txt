Rising borrowing costs, questions over returns from its OpenAI investment and uncertainty surrounding AI infrastructure spending have added to credit market concerns. ReutersThe five-year credit default swap (CDS) spread for SoftBank widened 22 basis points to 466 basis points. The cost of insuring against a potential default by Japanese technology investor SoftBank Group surged to its highest level in four years on Friday, as growing concerns over the profitability of artificial intelligence investments rattled global financial markets, Reuters reported. The five-year credit default swap (CDS) spread for SoftBank widened 22 basis points to 466 basis points, its highest level since October 2022, according to Markit data cited by Reuters.
